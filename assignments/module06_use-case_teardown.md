# Module 06 Assignment: Use-Case Teardown

*Course module: Real-World AI Agent Applications (Udemy Section 7)* · Time: 30-90 min

## Your task
Pick one real agent product you can use (coding assistant, deep-research feature, customer-support bot, browser agent).
Apply the six-question design review from Module 6:
1. What is the goal and who is the user? 2. What can it read? 3. What can it change? 4. Where does its knowledge come from? 5. Which actions need approval? 6. How would you measure success?
Then give it 3 test tasks (easy, medium, hard) and record success/failure.

## What to submit
A one-page teardown + test results + 2 improvement ideas.

## Check yourself
- Did you connect your answer to the concepts of Module 6?
- Could someone else repeat your steps from what you wrote?
