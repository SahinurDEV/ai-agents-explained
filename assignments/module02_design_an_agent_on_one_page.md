# Module 02 Assignment: Design an Agent on One Page

*Course module: Understanding AI Agents (Udemy Section 3)* · Time: 30-90 min

## Your task
Pick a real task from your work or life (e.g. "plan my weekly meals", "triage support emails").
1. Write the agent's goal in one sentence.
2. List its components: model, instructions, tools (3-5), memory, knowledge.
3. Draw the reason-act loop for one example request, step by step (at least 3 loop iterations).
4. Choose its autonomy level (suggest / act with approval / act alone) and justify it.
5. Answer: would a simple workflow or a chatbot be enough? Why or why not?

## What to submit
A one-page diagram (hand-drawn photo is fine) + short answers to 1-5.

## Check yourself
- Did you connect your answer to the concepts of Module 2?
- Could someone else repeat your steps from what you wrote?
