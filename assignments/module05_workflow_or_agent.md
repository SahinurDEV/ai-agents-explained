# Module 05 Assignment: Workflow or Agent?

*Course module: Workflows and Multi-Agent Systems (Udemy Section 6)* · Time: 30-90 min

## Your task
For each of these 4 tasks decide: fixed workflow, single agent, or multi-agent - and draw the flow:
(a) Convert uploaded invoices to spreadsheet rows. (b) Answer customer questions using docs and order lookups. (c) Write a market research report from web sources with a reviewer. (d) Send a reminder email every Monday.
Then for the multi-agent case, define each agent's role, tools, and the hand-off message format.

## What to submit
4 small diagrams + one paragraph per task justifying your choice.

- Did you connect your answer to the concepts of Module 5?
- Could someone else repeat your steps from what you wrote?
